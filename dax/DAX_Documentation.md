# Comprehensive DAX Measures & Business Intelligence Logic Guide

This document provides complete documentation for the DAX measures developed for the **LendingClub Credit Risk & Loan Portfolio Dashboard** in Microsoft Power BI.

---

## 1. Portfolio Capital & Volume Metrics

| Measure Name | DAX Expression | Business Definition & Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`Total Loan Applications`** | `COUNTROWS('Fact_Loans')` | Total loan agreements originated across the 2021--2024 operating window (50,000 retail facilities). |
| **`Total Funded Amount`** | `SUM('Fact_Loans'[funded_amount])` | Total dollar capital deployed across borrowers ($787.1M total exposure). |
| **`Average Loan Size`** | `DIVIDE([Total Funded Amount], [Total Loan Applications], 0)` | Average facility size per retail borrower ($15,742 benchmark). |
| **`Total Borrower Payments`** | `SUM('Fact_Loans'[total_payment])` | Cumulative principal, interest, and late charges collected to date. |

---

## 2. Credit Risk & Regulatory Loss Provisioning

| Measure Name | DAX Expression | Business Definition & Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`Total Defaulted Amount`** | `CALCULATE(SUM('Fact_Loans'[funded_amount]), 'Fact_Loans'[is_default] = 1)` | Dollar volume of facilities charged off or in non-accrual default status. |
| **`Default Rate %`** | `DIVIDE([Total Defaulted Amount], [Total Funded Amount], 0)` | Primary credit risk KPI (19.59% portfolio weighted average). Used in grade slicers. |
| **`Expected Credit Loss (EL)`** | `[Total Defaulted Amount] * 0.45` | Regulatory loss provision required under the **Basel-II IRB Framework** assuming 45% Loss Given Default (LGD). |
| **`Net Credit Loss`** | `[Total Defaulted Amount] - [Total Recoveries]` | Actual net charge-off balance after factoring in post-default asset recovery efforts. |
| **`Recovery Rate %`** | `DIVIDE([Total Recoveries], [Total Defaulted Amount], 0)` | Efficiency of post-charge-off collection agencies and asset realization. |

---

## 3. Underwriting Risk Profiling

| Measure Name | DAX Expression | Business Definition & Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`Weighted Average Interest Rate`** | `DIVIDE(SUMX('Fact_Loans', 'Fact_Loans'[int_rate] * 'Fact_Loans'[funded_amount]), [Total Funded Amount], 0) / 100` | Capital-weighted yield generated by the loan portfolio. |
| **`Weighted Average DTI`** | `DIVIDE(SUMX('Fact_Loans', 'Fact_Loans'[dti] * 'Fact_Loans'[funded_amount]), [Total Funded Amount], 0) / 100` | Borrower repayment capacity; high DTI (>25%) flags heightened sensitivity to rate hikes. |
| **`High Risk Exposure %`** | `DIVIDE(CALCULATE([Total Funded Amount], 'Dim_Grade'[grade] IN {"E", "F", "G"}), [Total Funded Amount], 0)` | Concentration of speculative-grade debt (Grades E, F, G) requiring enhanced capital reserves. |

---

## 4. Time Intelligence & Trend Forecasting

| Measure Name | DAX Expression | Business Definition & Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`Funded Amount YTD`** | `TOTALYTD([Total Funded Amount], 'Dim_Date'[date])` | Year-to-date cumulative capital origination volume. |
| **`Funded Amount YoY Growth %`** | `DIVIDE([Total Funded Amount] - [Funded Amount Prior Year], [Funded Amount Prior Year], 0)` | Year-over-year expansion rate of retail loan originations. |
| **`Default Rate MoM Change`** | `[Default Rate %] - CALCULATE([Default Rate %], DATEADD('Dim_Date'[date], -1, MONTH))` | Month-over-month acceleration or deceleration in credit defaults. |
